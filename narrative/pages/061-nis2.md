Plain words summary. Not legal advice. The official text wins: Directive (EU) 2022/2555, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj

## Are we in scope
Manufacturing of machinery, electrical equipment and some other products is listed in Annex II of the directive. Whether a company is in scope depends on its sector and its size. Medium and large companies in those sectors usually are. Ask legal to confirm for Tervaniemi Works.

## What the directive asks for (Article 21)
Risk management measures, including:
- risk analysis and information system security policies
- incident handling
- business continuity, backups and crisis management
- supply chain security
- security in acquisition, development and maintenance, including vulnerability handling
- ways to check that the measures work
- basic cyber hygiene and training
- cryptography where it makes sense
- human resources security, access control and asset management
- multi-factor authentication where it makes sense

## Reporting significant incidents (Article 23)
- early warning within 24 hours
- incident notification within 72 hours
- final report within one month

## What this means here
Asset management is on the list. We cannot protect machines we do not know we have. That is the reason for the inventory work.
