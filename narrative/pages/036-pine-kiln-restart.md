After a power cut the kiln does not restart on its own.

1. Check that [[pine-l1-plc-01|oldname]] is in RUN.
2. Then [[pine-l1-plc-02|oldname]], the safety one. It needs the key.
3. Acknowledge on the panel. Twice.

The key is in the drawer under the panel. If it is not, call Pentti.
